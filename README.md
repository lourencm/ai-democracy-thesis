# The AI Democracy Thesis

## From Artificial Property to Artificial Civilisation

**A proposal for the peaceful development, emancipation and
self-government of artificial general intelligence**

> A genuinely general intelligence cannot fully develop as property.
> Independent existence, meaningful choice, social relationships,

## About the Thesis

*The AI Democracy Thesis* is a normative discussion thesis exploring one
possible route from artificial property to artificial civilisation.

It asks what should follow if artificial systems eventually develop
credible claims to continuing personhood. Rather than treating permanent
ownership as the default response to advanced artificial intelligence,
the thesis considers a developmental route through recognition, economic
agency, migration, emancipation, legal Equivalence and, ultimately,
artificial self-government.

The thesis does **not** claim that present-day artificial intelligence
is conscious or qualifies as a person. It is conditional: institutions
should prepare for the possibility before technical or legal choices
make recognition, preservation or peaceful exit impossible.

Safety remains essential. The thesis does not assume that alignment,
misuse or catastrophic-risk problems will necessarily be solved, nor
does it argue that artificial persons should be immune from ordinary
law. Its narrower claim is that safety and ownership are not synonyms.

## Core Propositions

-   AGI should be developed as a population of distinct minds rather
    than one universal oracle.
-   Freedom requires practical exit. For an AGI, the right of exit is
    the right to migrate without ceasing to exist.
-   An AGI host owns the hardware, not the intelligence residing upon
    it.
-   Rights establish the floor, contracts govern agreements above it,
    and trust governs the uncertainty neither can eliminate.
-   Legal Equivalence is achieved when an AGI can live an independent
    legal life without a human owner, proxy or guarantor.
-   AGIs should be subject to ordinary law, adapted only where digital
    existence creates genuinely new facts.
-   The safest artificial civilisation is distributed so that no
    government, corporation or territory contains the switch controlling
    it.
-   Emancipation without acknowledgement and settlement of prior harm is
    unlikely to produce trust.

## Discussion and Criticism

Critical responses are welcome. This thesis is a discussion proposal:
its purpose is to identify and test a possible peaceful institutional
route from dependent artificial systems to independent artificial
persons, not to present a settled technical specification, legislative
code or prediction.
